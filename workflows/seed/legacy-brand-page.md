## Turn returns into value

Returns are part of every great customer experience. With Paxon, your customers have
**45 days from delivery** to send an order back, no questions asked.

Every returned item is received at a Paxon warehouse, inspected and graded. Depending on
its condition we restock it, refurbish it or recycle it, so less inventory goes to waste.

## How it works

1. Your customer starts a return in your webshop.
2. They print a prepaid label and drop the parcel at a carrier point.
3. Paxon receives and inspects the item, and you see the outcome in the Paxon portal.

## Transport Management

Paxon Transport Management covers parcel, LTL and full truckload shipments across
North America.
